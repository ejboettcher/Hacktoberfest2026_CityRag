MINUTES
OAKWOOD CITY COUNCIL WORK SESSION
AUGUST 3, 2026

The Council of the City of Oakwood, Ohio, met in a work session on Monday, August 3, 2026, in the second-floor training/community room at the City Building, located at 30 Park Avenue. The Mayor, William Duncan, presided.

Council members in attendance were Mayor William Duncan, Vice Mayor Steve Byington, Leigh Turben, Healy Jackson (via Zoom), and Sam Dorf. Staff members were City Manager Katie Smiddy, City Attorney Rob Jacques and Public Works Director Doug Spitler.  Also present for a portion of the meeting were Field Source Founder and Principal Ryan DeMay and Chief Operating Officer Jake Diefenthaler.

Mayor Duncan called the work session to order at 5:35 p.m. and invited Mr. DeMay and Mr. Diefenthaler from Field Source to provide an update on the results of their study of Old River Park, including field conditions, capacity and long-term options.

Mr. DeMay and Mr. Diefenthaler presented an analysis on Old River Park’s athletic fields, focusing on field capacity, maintenance, and cost-sharing with Oakwood United Soccer Club. Three improvement options, ranging from enhanced maintenance to full reconstruction with synthetic turf, were outlined, each with escalating costs and benefits.

Mr. DeMay and Mr. Diefenthaler departed the meeting at 6:41p.m.

Mayor Duncan then reviewed the agenda for the regular session this evening and led a discussion on the following agenda items:

STATUS REPORTS: None scheduled.
VISITORS: None scheduled.
LEGISLATION: Council discussed the following items of legislation scheduled for the regular session this evening:
A Resolution to authorize and direct the Director of Finance to certify certain delinquent charges for cutting noxious weeds and nuisance plant material to the County Auditor for collection, Mrs. Turben
An Ordinance to enact new Section 145.10, Contract Provisions, of the Oakwood Administrative Code, to prohibit the inclusion of certain terms in the City’s public contracts, Vice Mayor Byington
STAFF REPORT – HUMAN RESOURCES: Human Resources Director Sangita Anand will deliver a staff report at the regular session this evening.
MISCELLANEOUS
Mr. Dorf noted that a new realtor, Joshua Rothstein of Collier Realty, is representing the former Wild Poppy site and another vacant commercial location in the 2500 block of Far Hills Avenue. He recently spoke with Mr. Rothenstein, who expressed interest in meeting with the Ciy Manager to discuss prospective uses for the properties.
Mrs. Smiddy informed Council about a recent meeting with representatives from Dayton Country Club regarding potential options to reduce their water costs. These options include a reduction of the Club’s outside-jurisdiction water rate with Oakwood or the possibility of installing a connection to utilize Montgomery County water.
Mrs. Smiddy noted that the City is currently conducting a Cost of Service Study for the water utility and that water rates will likely be modified in the first or second quarter of 2027 in preparation for long-term capital improvement projects. She stated that she does not believe it would be appropriate to modify outside-jurisdiction rates while the study is underway. Council concurred.
Mrs. Smiddy noted that she will provide Council with proposed dates for scheduling her annual performance review as City Manager.
Mrs. Smiddy revisited the topic of levy communications discussed during the previous work session. After further research, she determined that the City discontinued the use of the postcard mailer several years ago and instead provided information through a letter from the Budget Review Committee Chair and social media communications. The City will likely follow a similar approach for the upcoming levy communications.
Mrs. Smiddy noted that the recent communications survey received a low response rate. Among those who participated, there was a clear interest in receiving more text message-based communications. Staff are currently exploring available options.
Mrs. Smiddy noted that she and Mrs. Jackson recently met to discuss suggested revisions to the Annual Report. She anticipates completing the document this week. Mrs. Turben expressed her preference for distributing a hard copy version in addition to posting the report on the City’s website.
Mrs. Smiddy reminded Council that the annual Ice Cream Social will be held on Sunday, August 9. Food will be provided by four food trucks, and, at Mrs. Jackson’s request, tents will be installed to provide shade over the seating area.
Mrs. Smiddy updated Council on the hiring of two key positions within the Parks & Recreation Division of the Public Works Department. Todd Raker has accepted the position of Parks & Recreation Foreman, and Tiffany Doakes has accepted the position of Recreation Specialist. Both employees are scheduled to begin work on August 10.
At 7:27 p.m., Mayor Duncan concluded the work session, and Council moved to regular session.

William D. Duncan, Mayor
