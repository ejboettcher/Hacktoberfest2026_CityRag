MINUTES
OAKWOOD CITY COUNCIL WORK SESSION
AUGUST 17, 2026

The Council of the City of Oakwood, Ohio, met in a work session on Monday, August 17, 2026, in the second-floor training/community room at the City Building, located at 30 Park Avenue. The Mayor, William Duncan, presided.

Council members present were Mayor William Duncan, Vice Mayor Steve Byington, Leigh Turben, Healy Jackson, and Sam Dorf. Staff present were City Manager Katie Smiddy, Public Safety Director Alan Hill, Law Director Rob Jacques, Public Works Director Doug Spitler, City Engineer/Assistant Public Works Director Chris Kuzma, Human Resources Director Sangita Anand, Finance Director Maralee Leonard and Clerk of Council Lori Stacel.

Mayor Duncan called the work session to order at 5:03 p.m. and invited city staff to provide updates on their respective departments.

Public Safety Department

Public Safety Director Alan Hill provided updates on several major initiatives, including the AXS wireline dispatch console upgrade, department-wide portable radio replacement, Engine 27 replacement, the Citizens Police Academy, the addition of a second electric patrol vehicle, leadership development, and the replacement of in-car and body-worn cameras.

Engineering/Public Works Department

City Engineer/Assistant Public Works Director Chris Kuza reviewed completed and ongoing infrastructure projects, including the 2026 asphalt repaving program, water main projects, facility improvements, and implementation of recommendations from the City’s water system studies. Council was also updated on the Lead and Copper Rule Improvements and the City’s long-term water system capital improvement program.

Public Works Director Doug Spitler provided updates on the CMMS (Computerized Maintenance Management System), a cooperative purchasing software program used to manage work orders; the Park Master Plan, supported by a $450,000 State of Ohio capital grant; and ongoing tree, mowing, and aeration service programs.

Finance Department

Finance Director Maralee Leonard reviewed ongoing modernization efforts, including digitization of accounts payable, migration of the City’s ERP (Enterprise Resource Program) system to the cloud, implementation of an employee portal and CityTax, enhanced budgeting and analytics tools, and development of a digital budget book.

Human Resources Department

Human Resources Director Sangita Anand provided an overview of its 2026 accomplishments and priorities, including recruitment and retention, employee development, labor relations, wellness initiatives, healthcare cost management, and organizational support.

Law Department and Planning & Zoning

Law Director Rob Jacques reviewed 2026 goals for the law department, including implementation of a cybersecurity program, transitioning the City Code to a searchable online format, potential Charter amendments, future updates to the Comprehensive Plan and Zoning Code, employee training, and continued support for code enforcement responsibilities.  He also presented current enforcement and permitting activity and discussed priorities related to proactive code enforcement, streamlining zoning permits, neighborhood maintenance, and rental registration compliance.

Council members had an opportunity to ask questions and discuss the various departmental projects, priorities, and future goals.

Refuse Code Enforcement

Mr. Dorf asked about refuse code enforcement related to trash in alleys and the possibility of imposing fees or fines. Mr. Jacques provided information regarding the applicable provisions of the Code and expressed hesitation about imposing fines.

Mayor Duncan shared that Abby Riedel is now serving as Chair of the Board of Health. He also noted that the Ohio Municipal League Annual Conference is scheduled for September 30 through October 2, 2026.

Mrs. Jackson shared that there is a 2026 Northeast Ohio Planning & Zoning Workshop on August 28, 2026.

The work session concluded at 8:10 p.m. and the meeting was adjourned.

William D. Duncan, Mayor

Lori Stacel,  Clerk of Council
